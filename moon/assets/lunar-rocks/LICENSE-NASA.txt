NASA Astromaterials3D scan derivatives

NASA Astromaterials3D, Lunar Sample Laboratory Facility, Acquisition & Curation Office; supported by PDART proposal15-PDART15_2-0041.

Source data are public domain according to NASA Astromaterials3D.

Original meshes and photographs: NASA Astromaterials3D.
These derivatives include mesh decimation, normalization, UV-preserving export, normal baking and bounded texture lighting correction.
The derivative albedo is a photographic approximation, not a calibrated measurement.
